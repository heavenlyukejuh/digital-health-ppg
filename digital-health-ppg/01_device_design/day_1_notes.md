# Day 1 — Project Foundation

## Why PPG?
- Widely used in ICU, ER, OR, wearables, home monitoring.
- Combines device physics + digital health analytics.
- Bridges clinical engineering and ML.

## What This Project Will Do
- Simulate a medical device subsystem (LED → photodiode → ADC).
- Clean and filter PPG signals.
- Identify artifacts like a clinical engineer would.
- Build ML models to classify good vs bad signals.
- Convert raw PPG to HR/SpO₂ like a wearable.

## What I Set Up Today
- Created repository structure.
- Defined project scope.
- Connected device engineering + digital health workflows.
- Prepared folders for each engineering domain.
